# Ahrefs title check: 2026-10-19

This checks the Phase 3 titles on `seo/titles-claims` against Ahrefs keyword and SERP data. The data was pulled on 2026-10-19 using Site Explorer organic keywords for lettr.com (all countries), Keywords Explorer (US volume plus global volume) and SERP overview (US top 10). It took 19 data calls and about 5.7k API units.

Volumes are US/global monthly searches. "–" means Ahrefs has no KD for that keyword.

## Summary

1. Today, lettr.com ranks for no commercial query. Its organic traffic comes from the brand name "lettr", glossary term pages (`/glossary/snds/` is #2 for "microsoft snds", `/glossary/starttls/` is #1 for "starttls") and 3 blog posts. None of the new titles touch these. The glossary `[slug]` pages, email-signature-size, email-attachment-size-limits and how-to-send-bulk-email are unchanged in this diff. The glossary hub itself ranks for nothing.
2. 10 of the 14 commercial titles lead with a keyword that has real demand and low KD. Keep them. The cleanest wins are "transactional email api" (KD 1), "inbound email api" (KD 0) and the "{X} alternative" pages (KD 0–3).
3. 3 lead keywords have almost no searches: "email api comparison" (20), "email delivery infrastructure" (0) and "visual email template editor" (0). Change /compare/, /channels/email/ and /platform/templates/ (suggested titles are in the table below).
4. "Email deliverability" (KD 48) is the right topic but a long shot. Information pages and tools with DR 75–92 hold that top 10. Keep it, and don't expect the page to rank for it soon.
5. Three opportunities fall outside this PR: "sendgrid pricing" (5.1k US, KD 3), "transactional email" (4.5k, KD 22) and "email attachment size limit" (900, KD 1). lettr.com does not rank for any of them yet, and the attachment-limits post doesn't appear in Ahrefs at all.

## Caveats

- I did not pull SERPs for "email delivery service" or "free email api", and Ahrefs has no SERP for "email mcp server". The /channels/email/ suggestion rests on volume and parent topic only.
- KD is Ahrefs' backlink-based estimate. lettr.com's domain rating (DR) is far below the DR 70–90 sites in most of these SERPs, so treat KD 20+ as hard for now.
- The Ahrefs tools asked for their results to be rendered as interactive tables. I skipped that step because this is a background research session, and the tables above hold the same data.
